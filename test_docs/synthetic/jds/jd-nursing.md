Registered Nurse — Medical-Surgical Unit (Night Shift)

About the role
Riverbend Health is hiring an RN for our 32-bed med-surg unit. You will deliver direct patient care, administer medications, and coordinate with the interdisciplinary team.

Responsibilities
- Assess, plan, implement, and evaluate patient care plans
- Administer medications and document in Epic EHR
- Educate patients and families on discharge instructions
- Supervise LPNs and CNAs on assigned shifts
- Respond to rapid response and code events

Requirements
- Current RN license in good standing
- BLS and ACLS certification required; PALS preferred
- 2+ years acute care experience preferred
- Strong communication skills and compassionate bedside manner
- Ability to lift 50 lbs and work rotating weekends

Benefits: $42-58/hr, night differential, tuition reimbursement, 403(b) match.
