Principal Platform Engineer — Global Streaming Company

About the job
We operate one of the largest video delivery platforms in the world, serving 240 million subscribers across 190 countries. The platform engineering group owns the paved road every product team builds on: compute, delivery pipelines, observability, and developer experience. This role sits at the center of that group, reporting to the VP of Infrastructure, and carries organization-wide technical influence without direct reports.

The team
Platform engineering is 85 people across six sub-teams: Compute, Delivery, Observability, Developer Experience, Capacity, and Reliability. The Compute team runs the container platform; Delivery owns build and deploy; Observability runs metrics, logging, and tracing at petabyte scale; Developer Experience maintains the golden paths; Capacity does forecasting and procurement; Reliability owns incident tooling and the on-call program. You will float across all six, spending roughly a quarter of your time on whichever has the gnarliest problem.

What you will do
- Design multi-region failover strategies for services handling 30M requests per second at peak
- Lead the migration from our homegrown scheduler to Kubernetes across 40,000 nodes
- Define SLOs with product teams and build the error-budget tooling that enforces them
- Own the internal developer portal and golden-path templates used by 2,300 engineers
- Mentor senior engineers and run the weekly architecture review forum
- Partner with the FinOps team to cut compute spend without hurting tail latency
- Drive incident reviews that actually change systems rather than assign blame
- Evaluate emerging runtimes and champion adoption where the numbers justify it
- Write the design memos that set direction for the next three years of platform work
- Sit on the promotion committee for staff-plus engineering candidates

What we look for
- 12+ years building and operating distributed systems at serious scale
- Deep fluency in Go or Java plus working Python for tooling and automation
- Production Kubernetes expertise including operators and custom controllers
- Strong SQL skills and well-argued opinions about when NoSQL is the wrong answer
- Experience with AWS at organizational scale, including negotiating reservations and committed-use discounts
- Fluency with CI/CD design: build graphs, hermetic builds, progressive delivery, automated rollback
- Docker and container runtime internals beyond the happy path — cgroups, namespaces, image formats
- Excellent written communication — our culture runs on memos, not meetings
- A track record of disagreeing productively with principal-level peers and changing your mind in public
- Comfort operating systems you did not build and inheriting decisions you would not have made

Nice to have
- Contributions to open-source infrastructure projects we already run — Envoy, Prometheus, Argo, Cilium
- Experience with edge compute and CDN architecture at consumer scale
- Familiarity with ML serving infrastructure and GPU scheduling
- Prior work in media, gaming, or real-time entertainment
- Experience presenting at infrastructure conferences or writing publicly about production engineering

How we work
The platform group is remote-first with quarterly onsites in Amsterdam. We ship behind feature flags, review each other's designs in writing before any meeting is booked, and treat operational load as a first-class engineering problem with a budget and a burn-down chart. On-call is compensated, humane, and shared eight ways per rotation; if you are paged twice in a night the morning is yours. Career growth here does not require becoming a manager; our principal track is real, funded, and currently occupied by four people who still write code every week.

A typical quarter
In your first quarter you might: spend three weeks embedded with the Delivery team unpicking why deploy lead time regressed from eleven minutes to forty; write the memo proposing a build-cache architecture and defend it at the review forum; pair with a staff engineer on Capacity to model the traffic implications of a new markets launch; and close the quarter presenting the platform roadmap to two hundred engineers at the all-hands. None of this is hypothetical — each item is something the current principals did last quarter.

Compensation and benefits
$210,000 - $290,000 base depending on experience and location, annual equity refreshers, 6% 401(k) match, 20 weeks parental leave regardless of how you became a parent, a $4,000 annual learning budget, and a sabbatical program that unlocks at year five. Relocation to Amsterdam is available but never required.

Interview process
One recruiter screen, one systems design deep dive, one code pairing session in your language of choice, one values conversation with two future peers, and a final architecture presentation on a system you have actually operated — not a whiteboard fiction. The loop takes roughly three weeks end to end and we compensate your time if you complete the full loop and do not receive an offer.

Equal opportunity
We hire adults and treat them like adults. If you need accommodations at any stage, tell us and they will happen without ceremony.
